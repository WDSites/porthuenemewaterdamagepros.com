Oxnard water problems we see most

Storm water is the headline problem. The December 2023 storm hit Oxnard hardest. Gauges downtown logged 3.18 inches between midnight and 1 a.m., per the Associated Press, and the city told residents to stay off flooded streets. Homes near low corners and storm drain inlets took on water. See flood damage restoration for how crews handle it.

Away from storms, the common calls are supply line failures, water heater leaks, washing machine hoses and slow plumbing leaks inside older stucco homes. About 54 percent of Oxnard's housing units are owner-occupied, so many repairs run through a homeowner's policy. Check your policy, and tell your insurer about the date you first noticed the leak.

Before we arrive in Oxnard

Stop the source

Close the fixture shut-off or the main water valve. Oxnard's Water Division has a distribution operator on duty 24/7 for water service emergencies if the street valve is the issue.

Cut power with care

Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water. Otherwise call 911 or the utility first.

Lift and protect

Raise furniture legs, pick up rugs and move papers and electronics to a dry shelf.

Keep clear and call

Keep people and pets out of any water that came from outside or a drain, then call the 24/7 line.

Oxnard facts that affect water damage

The City of Oxnard says its tap water is fairly hard to hard, averaging about 285 parts per million, and a Water Distribution Operator is on duty 24/7 for service emergencies. Hard water leaves mineral scale at old fittings, which can show up as slow drips under sinks.

The Santa Clara River separates Oxnard from Ventura, and the city sits on the Oxnard Plain, which is flat farmland and coastal lowland. Flat ground drains slowly after heavy rain, so check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask the city building department about permit requirements for rebuild work.

Sources: City of Oxnard Water Division water quality page and Associated Press report on December 21, 2023.
