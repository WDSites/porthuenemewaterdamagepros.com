Port Hueneme Water Damage Pros takes calls about water damage and mold for Port Hueneme and nearby Ventura County cities. Our line is answered 24/7. We connect each call with licensed and insured restoration professionals who handle extraction, drying, cleanup and repair.

How we work

Every job begins with a phone call where the dispatcher listens, gives safety steps and explains what happens next. You get the scope and price up front. We do not post made-up reviews, ratings or job counts, and we do not promise arrival times.

We refer to ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage restoration and S520 for mold remediation as published industry standards, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to them. We do not claim certifications that we cannot show.

Where we are

Our address is 501 W Channel Islands Blvd, Port Hueneme, CA 93041. Channel Islands Boulevard is the main business street on the north side of the city. Naval Base Ventura County sits close by, as does the Port of Hueneme, and the Hueneme Pier is a short drive toward the beach.
