Hillside, river and harbor: Ventura's three water problems

Ventura is the Ventura County seat, with about 110,800 residents in the 2020 census. It is a long city between the hills and the ocean, with the Ventura River down its west side and the Santa Clara River on its southern edge. Each one drives a different kind of water damage.

Seasonal problems in Ventura

Heavy winter rain is the clearest seasonal driver. Most of Ventura's rain comes from December through February, and hillside homes can see runoff reach garages and lower levels. When the ground is saturated, roof leaks and clogged gutters join the list. After the 2017 Thomas Fire burned hillside neighborhoods, residents learned that cleared slopes shed water faster.

Ventura has a bigger share of homeowners than Oxnard or Port Hueneme, with about 55 percent of housing units owner-occupied in 2020. That means more claims and more questions about coverage. Ask your insurer about the cause and timeline, and document every wet room before cleanup.

Before we arrive in Ventura

Shut off the source

Close the fixture valve or the main water valve. If the line outside is leaking, call your provider from the bill.

Power safely

Switch off wet-area breakers only if the panel is dry. If water touches outlets, call the utility or 911.

Raise belongings

Lift furniture legs, move rugs and electronics, and keep clothing and papers off wet floors.

Stay clear and call

Keep pets and children out of storm or drain water, then call the 24/7 line.

Ventura facts that affect water damage

Ventura Water says the city relies on local sources, including Lake Casitas, groundwater basins and recycled water, and the city is crossed by the Ventura River and bounded on the south by the Santa Clara River. River flooding is a long-term risk to check on the FEMA map for your address.

The city is also a coastal city, with a historic pier dating to 1872 that was heavily damaged by a 1995 storm and later rebuilt. Old downtown buildings can have aging plumbing, so ask the city building department about permits when repairing older units.
